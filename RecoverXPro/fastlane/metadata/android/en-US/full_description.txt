RecoverX is a privacy-first Android photo recovery utility built around real, verifiable operations.

Recover photos exposed through Android MediaStore, inspect system trash where the device exposes it, scan a folder you explicitly select, export recovered files, and carve JPEG/PNG images from a user-provided storage image or dump file.

RecoverX does not invent scan percentages, fake recovery results, or promise universal recovery of permanently deleted data.

Core features:
• MediaStore image discovery
• System-trash discovery on supported Android versions
• User-selected folder scan through Android Storage Access Framework
• Safe export to a user-selected folder
• System-consent restore for MediaStore trash items
• Deep forensic JPEG/PNG carving from a storage image or dump
• Arabic and English interface
• Local-first privacy
